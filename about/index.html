简介Biography

苏振坤，对外经济贸易大学国际经济贸易学院博士研究生，硕士毕业于山东师范大学经济学院。研究兴趣为法经济学、公司信息披露与制度经济学，关注司法改革、制度型开放与数据要素市场化等制度变迁对企业行为与产业链韧性的影响。研究成果发表于 Economics & Politics、《产业经济研究》、《经济与管理评论》等期刊。

Zhenkun Su is a PhD student at the School of International Trade and Economics, University of International Business and Economics (UIBE), and holds a master's degree in economics from Shandong Normal University. The research lies at the intersection of law and economics and corporate finance, studying how judicial reform, institutional opening-up and data-market reforms shape corporate disclosure, innovation and supply-chain resilience.
